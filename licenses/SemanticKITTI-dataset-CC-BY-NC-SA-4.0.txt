SemanticKITTI dataset
http://www.semantic-kitti.org/

The SemanticKITTI dataset (LiDAR scans, point labels, voxel ground truth) is
NOT distributed in this repository; it is downloaded separately by the user.
However, it is the training/evaluation data for this project, and the released
pretrained checkpoint is a derivative (adaptation) of it. Its license therefore
governs that checkpoint and any predictions derived from the dataset.
